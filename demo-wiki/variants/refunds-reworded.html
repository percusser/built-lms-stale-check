Refund policy

Owner: Legal · Last updated October 20, 2026

How long customers have

A customer has 30 days, counted from the day the order was delivered, to return most items and get their money back in full.

What to ask for

  • Some proof of purchase. A receipt, an order number or the card they paid with all count.
  • The item itself, still resalable and with the tags on.

Paying the refund

We always refund to the payment method the customer originally used. It reaches them in 5 to 7 business days from when the item gets to us.

If the customer has no proof of purchase, they get store credit at the current selling price, not a refund.

Never returnable

  • Gift cards
  • Rope and chain cut to a custom length
  • Fuel canisters that have been opened

Shipping costs

We keep the original shipping charge, except when the item was damaged on arrival or we shipped the wrong thing.
