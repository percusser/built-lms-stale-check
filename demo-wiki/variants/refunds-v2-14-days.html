Refund policy

Owner: Legal · Last updated November 2, 2026

Return window

Customers can return most items for a full refund within 14 days of the delivery date.

What the customer needs

  • Proof of purchase: the receipt, the order number, or the card used for the purchase.
  • The item in resalable condition, with its tags attached.

How the refund is paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method. The customer sees the money within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the item.

Without proof of purchase, offer store credit for the current selling price instead of a refund.

Items that cannot be returned

  • Gift cards
  • Custom-cut rope and chain
  • Opened fuel canisters

Shipping costs

Original shipping costs are not refunded, unless the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.
