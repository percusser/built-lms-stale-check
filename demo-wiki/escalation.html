Escalation process

Owner: Support Operations · Last updated August 28, 2026

When to escalate

Tier 1 agents own the ticket first. Escalate to Tier 2 after two failed fixes: two different troubleshooting steps that did not solve the problem.

Escalate right away, without trying fixes first, when the customer reports a safety issue with a product.

How to escalate

  1. Set the ticket priority and add the tag tier2.
  2. Write a handoff note: what the customer reported, both fixes you tried, and what happened.
  3. Tell the customer that a specialist will take over and when they will hear back.

Response targets

Escalation typeFirst response from Tier 2
StandardWithin 4 business hours
Safety issueWithin 1 hour, any time of day

Refund approvals

Refunds over $500 need approval from a support manager before they are issued.
